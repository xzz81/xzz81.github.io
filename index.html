About

I am a Research Assistant at Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology. I research unified multimodal models from the perspectives of training and inference, investigating whether different modalities are genuinely unified rather than merely supported within the same model. I also work on recursive self-improvement (RSI) in embodied agents.

My long-term vision is to explore how understanding, reasoning, generation, and action can improve one another within a unified model, with AGI as a distant dream.

I have access to substantial GPU resources and am keen to explore open questions across this system's components and their interactions. If you are interested in exchanging ideas or collaborating, feel free to contact me.
